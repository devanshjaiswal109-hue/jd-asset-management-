FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum tenure for FD Plan?

The minimum tenure for the Fixed Deposit plan is 3 months. You can choose to invest for longer durations as well.

Are the returns guaranteed in FD Plan?

Yes! The FD Plan offers fixed returns of 9-10% per annum with 100% capital protection. No market risk involved.

Are the return ranges for Stock plans guaranteed?

No. Any return range shown for stock market plans is not guaranteed. Investments in securities are subject to market risks.

What are the call support timings?

Our dedicated call support is available from Monday to Friday, 11AM to 5PM. You can reach out to us during these hours for any queries.
